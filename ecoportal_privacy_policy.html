This Privacy Policy describes how information may be processed when you visit or use EcoPortal. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will seek that consent separately.

The information processed depends on the features enabled and how the service is used. It may include:

Account and authentication information

  • Name, email address, profile information, and account identifiers.
  • User roles and information needed to authenticate users and maintain sessions.

Organization and client information

  • Organization names, client account details, and business contact information entered by authorized users.
  • Relationships between client organizations, consultants, and projects.

Project and compliance information

  • Audit project names, descriptions, statuses, deadlines, and progress.

Users should only submit information they are authorized to provide and should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information.
